## Formula Legends 1.0

An F1 racing game for the PS Vita across four eras: 1980 wing cars, 2010 V8s, 2016 hybrids and
2025 ground-effect cars, each with its own ten teams, ten circuits, career and championship.

### Install
1. **FormulaLegends.vpk**: install with VitaShell.
2. **FormulaLegends-music.zip** (once): copy it to the Vita, open it in VitaShell, select all,
   copy, and paste at the root of `ux0:`. It unpacks to `ux0:data/FormulaLegends/DATA/music/`.
   Later updates only need the new VPK.

### In this version
- Pick your era at team headquarters, each with its own theme; quick race, championship and
  career follow that era
- Button steering setting (Quick / Smooth / Extra smooth) for d-pad driving, Smooth by default
- Main menu: tap the screen's top corners to skip songs
- Hills and city skylines on the horizon, finer terrain at Interlagos, Spa and Red Bull Ring,
  cleaned-up barriers and trackside objects
